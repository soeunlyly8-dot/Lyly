Our Story & Heritage

Discover the passion, tradition, and global fusion behind every plate served at Krama & Spice.

Rooted in Khmer Tradition

Founded with a deep love for Cambodian culture, Krama & Spice takes its name from the traditional Khmer checkered scarf (Krama) and the rich, aromatic spice blends that define our culinary legacy.

Every recipe we serve has been passed down through generations, combining fresh ingredients, vibrant herbs, and traditional Kroeung pastes to bring the genuine taste of Cambodia straight to your table.

A Touch of European Elegance

While our heart beats firmly in Southeast Asia, our culinary horizon extends westward. At Krama & Spice, we embrace the finesse of European gastronomy, blending classical continental techniques with local tropical flavors.

From carefully crafted reduction sauces and artisanal baking approaches to expertly paired continental dishes, we curate a unique dining adventure where timeless European refinement harmonizes gracefully with the bold, earthy spices of the East.

What We Stand For

Fresh Ingredients

We source local, high-quality vegetables, river fish, and meats daily to guarantee authentic texture and peak flavor in every dish.

Authentic Recipes

No shortcuts taken. We honor time-tested cooking techniques like handmade curry pastes and slow-simmered herbal broths.

Warm Hospitality

Embodying the gentle spirit of Cambodia, our team welcomes every guest like family into a cozy, heritage-inspired atmosphere.
